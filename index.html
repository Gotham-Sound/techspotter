Know every scene before the day.

TechSpotter reads your screenplay PDF and builds the breakdown: who speaks in every scene, who is present without lines, and what the Sound and Video departments need ready before anyone is on the clock.

🧮A matrix built for correction. Strict parsing that shows its work: every refused cue is one click from the grid, every claim reveals its source page on hover, every cell obeys your click.
🔊Department spotting. Singing and playback, phone calls with their far ends, sounds characters react to, phone screens, TVs, and played video. Evidence attached, dismiss per moment.
📤Exports that travel. .sceneline for the tool family, CSV and XLSX for everyone else, print for the table. Drop a .sceneline back in to resume a reviewed show instantly.
NO SCRIPT EVER LEAVES YOUR DEVICE. NOTHING IN THE CLOUD. EVER. All parsing happens in this browser tab. Zero network requests after the page loads: no uploads, no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry. Verify it yourself: open your browser's network tab and drop a script.

Designed as part of Gotham Sound's table read system; we think it has broader use across the general production community, so here it is. And if you are interested in having Gotham Sound record video and audio for your table read, more info is available here.
